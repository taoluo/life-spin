# About this page
Only violations of rules LifeLoop itself made: unknown project statuses, area links pointing
nowhere or at a page that is not an area, and malformed dates or priorities. Arbitrary attributes,
folder conventions and general link health are not LifeLoop's business — for those, see
[[Library/Std/Pages/Maintenance]], which reports broken and ambiguous links across the whole
space.

Observations about projects — nothing actionable, waiting only, untouched for weeks — are not
violations and live on [[Library/LifeLoop/Pages/Projects]] instead.

This page reports and never fixes.
